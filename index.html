Domi

Whether it is the furnace filter nobody remembers, the warranty in a drawer somewhere, or the question of who serviced the water heater last and when, Domi keeps the whole house in order. Ask it anything. It already knows your home.

Meet
Domvara

Domvara Home Intelligence was founded by William Kowaleski, a locomotive engineer and first-time tech founder. He is building Domi for the same reason most homeowners would: the house keeps asking questions, and the answers are scattered across a filing cabinet, three apps and someone's memory.

Our vision
✦ ✦ ✦
“The homeowner stays in control.”

Today Domi helps you manage your home. Tomorrow it becomes a home intelligence layer that can coordinate services, connect with smart-home systems and preserve the history of the house. The decisions stay yours.
